# Product Overview

## One-Line Pitch

CodeQuity is an AI-governed funding launchpad where startup capital is released through Casper proof rails only when milestone traction is proven.

## The Problem

Early-stage funding still depends heavily on narrative, warm introductions, and manual follow-up. That creates three problems:

- Investors release capital before they have enough proof of execution.
- Founders with real technical progress lose to better-networked teams.
- Accelerators and grant programs spend too much time manually checking milestone progress.

In a global hackathon or startup ecosystem, this is especially visible. Many teams look good in a pitch deck. Fewer teams keep shipping.

## The Solution

CodeQuity connects three layers:

- **AI diligence:** Scores startup traction and explains the decision.
- **Milestone funding:** Converts score thresholds into release conditions.
- **Casper settlement:** Records deposit and release proof through wallet-signed testnet transactions.

The result is a launchpad where capital is tied to execution, not charisma.

## Users

| User | What They Need | CodeQuity Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder | Raise without relying only on network access | Prove traction continuously and unlock milestone capital |
| Investor | Deploy capital with downside protection | Fund teams while keeping release conditions objective |
| Accelerator | Manage cohort grants and follow-on funding | Automate milestone checks and keep records auditable |
| Ecosystem fund | Support builders transparently | Track capital, progress, and proof from one dashboard |

## Product Modules

### Landing Page

Explains the product promise and directs users into the launchpad flow.

### Investor Dashboard

Shows funding rounds, startup opportunities, transaction context, and investor workflow.

### Startup Profiles

Shows company profile, wallet readiness, GitHub/product signals, AI due diligence, and raise readiness.

### Round Creation

Guided flow for choosing a startup, setting CSPR amount, defining milestone thresholds, and signing with Casper Wallet.

### Round Detail

The main proof page. It shows:

- Milestone tracker.
- Current score and release eligibility.
- AI reasoning.
- Casper deposit and release references.
- Escrow mode and wallet/contract identifiers.

### Admin/Approval

Supports investor approval and trusted setup workflows for the demo environment.

## Why This Can Win

The submission is not just a dashboard. It is a coherent protocol story:

1. AI is used in a mission-critical workflow, not only for chat.
2. Casper is part of the funding settlement and proof path.
3. The product has a clear customer and revenue model.
4. The demo has a concrete end-to-end flow.
5. The project can grow into a real fund, grant, or accelerator operating system.

## Success Criteria

A judge should be able to answer these questions within five minutes:

- What problem is CodeQuity solving?
- Why is Casper needed?
- What does the AI agent decide?
- What does the wallet sign?
- How does the platform make money?
- What part of the demo proves the system works?
