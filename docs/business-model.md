# Business Model

## Business Thesis

CodeQuity becomes the operating system for milestone-based startup funding. The wedge is Web3 ecosystems, hackathons, grant programs, accelerators, and angel syndicates because these groups already fund in stages and need transparent progress tracking.

The long-term business is a venture infrastructure platform: diligence, capital release, reporting, and portfolio monitoring.

## Primary Revenue Streams

| Stream | Buyer | Pricing Idea | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Success fee | Investors, ecosystem funds | 0.5% to 2% of released capital | CodeQuity is paid only when funding actually moves |
| SaaS subscription | Accelerators, grant programs, angel groups | Monthly plan by cohort/fund size | Customers need dashboards, approvals, reporting, and proof records |
| AI diligence reports | Investors and funds | Per-report credits or monthly bundle | Investors pay for faster screening and memo generation |
| API access | Venture platforms and ecosystem tools | Usage-based score/memo/signal calls | External platforms can embed CodeQuity scoring |
| White-label launchpad | Universities, L1/L2 ecosystems, corporate venture | Setup fee plus subscription | Larger programs want their own branded funding workflow |

## Why Customers Pay

### Investors

- Reduce manual diligence time.
- Release capital in stages.
- Keep audit records for LPs and internal review.
- Discover technical founders earlier.

### Founders

- Raise based on execution.
- Show objective proof to investors.
- Avoid repeated manual updates.
- Build reputation through measurable progress.

### Accelerators and Grant Programs

- Track cohort progress.
- Automate milestone checks.
- Make grant releases transparent.
- Produce reports for sponsors or ecosystem foundations.

## Go-To-Market

### Phase 1: Hackathons and Builder Grants

Target programs that already distribute small milestone grants. CodeQuity helps them rank projects, release funds, and show proof.

### Phase 2: Accelerators and Angel Syndicates

Add cohort dashboards, investor memos, and portfolio monitoring.

### Phase 3: Ecosystem Fund Infrastructure

Offer white-label launchpads for L1/L2 ecosystems, university entrepreneurship programs, and corporate venture teams.

### Phase 4: API Platform

Expose score, memo, and signal APIs for other venture tools and agents.

## Competitive Advantage

- Combines AI diligence with wallet-signed funding actions.
- Makes Casper settlement visible in the product, not hidden in backend logs.
- Gives both founder and investor value.
- Starts with a narrow wedge but expands into fund operations.

## Metrics To Track

- Total capital committed.
- Total capital released.
- Number of funded startups.
- Percentage of milestones released on time.
- AI report usage.
- Investor activation rate.
- Founder profile completion rate.
- Cohort/fund retention.

## Monetization Roadmap

2. Add paid accelerator dashboards.

4. Add API keys and usage limits.
5. Add white-label deployment packages.
6. Add enterprise compliance and reporting modules.
